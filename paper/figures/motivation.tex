\caption{Motivation of ReCA-SAM3 through representative adaptation locations. (a) CAT-Seg and RSKT-Seg aggregate image--text matching representations before task decoding. (b) SegEarth-OV3 fuses decoded semantic and instance responses and applies presence filtering. (c) ProC-SAM3 constructs prompts offline and calibrates responses; ROSE-SAM3 selects prompts using frozen responses on unlabeled images and refines semantic responses with instance evidence. These are alternative prompt-preparation strategies, not two stages of one method. (d) ReCA-SAM3 learns RemoteCLIP-guided cross-prompt and spatial aggregation of SAM 3 conditional features before frozen decoding. Q/K denotes attention queries and keys; conditional features supply the values and receive updates. SDF denotes Semantic--Detail Fusion; its skip inputs and detailed spatial branches are omitted here. Snowflakes mark frozen parameters. Gray image tiles are placeholders, and feature slices are schematic. The panels are not mutually exclusive; (b) and (c) are training-free, whereas (d) uses supervised adaptation.}
