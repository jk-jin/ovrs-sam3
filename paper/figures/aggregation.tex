\caption{RemoteCLIP-guided feature aggregation. (a) One block adds a cross-prompt increment and then a fused spatial increment to conditional features; the two plus signs denote these residual additions. Final LayerNorm (LN) is applied once after the complete block stack. (b) Cross-class attention connects the same spatial position across all prompts. Independently normalized semantic guidance and features construct queries and keys (Q/K); values (V) come only from normalized features. Slices and prompt relations are schematic, not measured attention weights. (c) Parallel 36-by-36, 24-by-24 and 12-by-12 grids contain 9, 4 and 1 regular windows, each of size 12-by-12. Purple semantic and green feature-pyramid-network (FPN) guidance are resized and normalized per scale for Q/K. The linked green modules share pre-normalization, Q/K/V and output projections, and relative position bias within a block; different blocks have independent parameters. Attention increments are aligned to 36-by-36, multiplied by scalar weights and summed at $\Sigma$. Matching w1, w2 and w3 tags identify the same Softmax outputs for the three scales, respectively; these learned scalars have no image-dependent input. The fused increment returns to the spatial residual in (a).}
